A 50-Kilometre Frame

The sample contains 95 records from 2014 to 2025, with 833 people recorded dead or missing. Each supplied coordinate falls within 50 km of a fixed reference point on Lampedusa.

Coordinates may locate an incident, rescue, recovery or arrival. Some conflict with the location description. The original coordinates are retained; individual notes appear on hover.

Why Lampedusa?

Lampedusa provides a fixed point from which to read these records. The sample includes journeys from Tunisia and Libya, rescues at sea, and deaths recorded after arrival.

One Crossing Among Many

Sfax appears as a departure point in many records. This diagram shows geographic context, not reconstructed journeys. The sample also includes departures elsewhere and records whose exact location is uncertain.

12 Years

Each disc uses the same distance and size scales. Recorded losses fluctuate from year to year, but the annual average in 2022–2025 is higher than in 2014–2021. This describes the records in this sample, not the probability of dying on a crossing.

Radius represents the supplied coordinate’s distance from the reference point. Mark size represents recorded deaths and disappearances. Direction and movement are not actual routes. Hover over a mark for its date and location description.
