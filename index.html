Only 50 Kilometers Away

From 2014 to 2025, records located within 50 kilometers of Lampedusa counted 833 people dead or missing. Some disappeared at sea. Others died after reaching land. Being close to the island did not always mean reaching safety.

Why Lampedusa?

Lampedusa lies about 190 kilometers from Sfax, Tunisia, and 205 kilometers from Sicily. Though closer to North Africa, it is part of Italy. For people seeking safety or a new life, the island offers a relatively short crossing to European soil.

A Route Toward Europe

For people leaving Tunisia, Lampedusa can be a first arrival point, with hopes of continuing farther into Europe. But even this relatively short crossing carries dangers. Boats can capsize, and exposure or a lack of essential care can turn the journey fatal.

12 Years

The losses rise and fall, with a heavier toll in recent years. More people were recorded dead or missing in 2024 than in any other year shown here. The number fell in 2025, but remained higher than in any year before 2022.
